## Reproducing any number

* Full suite, canonical order: `needle-board run N -- bash .auto/measure.sh` (inside the wrapper, so
  `$FLASH_PORT`/`$SERIAL_PORT` and the board lock are set). Restricted screen: `AUTO_GROUPS=primary`.
  A repeat of an already-measured image needs `AUTO_ALLOW_REPEAT=1` plus a reason.
* Host quality gates: `bash .auto/checks.sh` (host 19/19, fidelity probe, prefix isolation).
* CQ2 kernel differential (per tree): hoist the shape sweep in **that tree's own** `kbench.c`
  (the two lines in `.auto/exp96/kbench.c.sweep-hoisted`), build a throwaway dir with
  `-DNEEDLE_KBENCH=ON -DNEEDLE_KBENCH_ASM=1`, and look for
  `KB NUM ... kernel=tie1n exact=768/768 bitexact=1`.
* Behavioural capture: `serial_api.py` on the board's **own** console node inside its lock, then
  `demo/capture.py` unchanged (it speaks HTTP on 127.0.0.1:8081). Kill the `serial_api.py` children by
  PID afterwards - `pkill -f needle-api` misses them and the console stays claimed.
* Board attach (any status/request work): `tools/serial_api.py`'s `Device` with the constructor kept
  **closed** and DTR/RTS false before and after the open. Any other open discipline resets the chip and
  can leave it in ROM download mode, which looks exactly like a dead board.

## Closed by measurement (do not re-open without a changed premise)

* **uint16 offset stream (CV3W)** - 4x the packed bytes against a bus already ~69 % utilised.
* **FP32 norm sidecar** - +6.25 % bytes = +5.1 ms vs 1.4 ms of instruction saving.
* **120 MHz octal memory** - +3.51 % real, vendor-blocked: IDF refuses the temperature timing retune
  on this flash model (`ESP_ERR_NOT_SUPPORTED`).
* **assertion-level RAM** - +8,248 B but neutral on speed, and the residency idea it was meant to fund
  needs 3.59 MB/token.
* **phi delivery** - 9.3 % of the phase, needs ~18 KB/core against ~12 KB free.
* spill class, cache-config maxima (S3 Kconfig), spurious wakeups, wide-load QK, kron2 wide,
  PSRAM/software prefetch, split granularity, spin budgets, kernel-outline on the PVR/QK shippable
  body, console-echo and RoPE micro-hunts - all measured null or negative with the mechanism recorded.

## The one item needing the owner

All trees fail exactly the two heldout cases above. Evidence says **not arithmetic**: a pure transport
change (lossless RX ring, engine byte-unchanged) flips precisely that pair; the host produces the same
answer the device does on those prompts; the behaviour is deterministic and order-independent within an
image; a 10-request soak shows ordinary prompts are repeat-deterministic (5/5). **Options:** keep the
ring and re-baseline/replace those two cases; drop the ring (losing the >128-byte request fix and the
20-case suite); or keep them as blockers and ship nothing.
